# Visual placement contract

Chapter Writing owns story development, prose, Anshul style, formatting and internal humanizing. Visual Placement performs none of those passes again.

Create separate Artifact B by faithfully copying passing Artifact A reader-facing content and native formatting. Preserve every chapter word, heading, paragraph boundary, approved section order, icon, bullet, emphasis run, evidence attribution, citation, number, caveat and fixed punchline. Remove only named workflow appendices and execution audit material.

Insert only exact-source images with explicit human KEEP decisions. Use the mapped subsection and place a figure immediately after the paragraph explaining its relevant idea, at an existing paragraph boundary. Never separate a claim from its supporting explanation. If the mapped position is ambiguous, use the closest supported paragraph boundary within that subsection and record the reason separately; do not rewrite or move paragraphs to accommodate it.

Add sequential figure numbers, concise evidence-faithful captions, source attribution and accessibility descriptions. Preserve image proportions, scale for readability and adjust only local figure spacing or page breaks needed for placement. Never crop away evidence or change chapter typography, bold, italics, bullets or headings.

No fresh research, replacement images, generated/redrawn visuals, prose/style/humanizer pass or new preview labels. Honor EXCLUDE. Any PENDING blocks execution. Account for exact-source failures with a specific reason in the separate report, without substitutes.

Verify B against A after removing named appendices and accounting for figure/caption/accessibility additions. Reader prose and native formatting must match. Inspect figure placement, legibility, caption/source association and ordering. Writing/emphasis feedback returns to Chapter Writing; image-placement/layout/caption feedback can be addressed here.
